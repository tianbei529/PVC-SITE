Physical properties

Tensile strength ≥ 12 MPa, flexural modulus ≥ 800 MPa, water absorption < 0.2%, Shore D hardness 45–55. Full TDS available on request.

02

Working & processing

Can be sawed, routed, drilled, screwed, heat-bent (110–130 °C), solvent-welded and laminated. No special tooling required — standard woodworking equipment works.

Applications

Where PVC foam board is used

Because it is waterproof, rot-proof and termite-proof, PVC foam board replaces plywood and MDF in wet, humid and outdoor environments — and replaces acrylic and aluminium in cost-sensitive signage.

Signage & Display

UV-printed boards, billboards, exhibition stands, POP displays and 3D letters.

Furniture & Cabinetry

Bathroom vanities, kitchen cabinet doors, wardrobe panels, shelving and drawers.

Interior Fit-Out

Wall cladding, ceiling panels, partitions, decorative screens and shop fitting.

Industrial & Construction

Concrete formwork, chemical tanks lining, machine guards and cleanroom panels.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

PVC foam board (also called expanded PVC sheet, forex board or PVC foam sheet) is a rigid, lightweight sheet made from polyvinyl chloride resin with a fine closed-cell foam structure. It is 100% waterproof, lead-free, and can be cut, routed, screwed and heat-bent using standard woodworking tools. It is widely used for signage, furniture, cabinetry and construction.

Celuka board has a hard, dense outer skin with a uniform micro-cell core, which gives a very smooth surface and high rigidity — the best choice for printing and signage. Free foam board has more uniform density from face to core, making it easier to cut, route and screw; it is also more cost-effective and is preferred for furniture and interior fit-out.
